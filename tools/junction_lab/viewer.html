Simulator Configuration & Environment
Research Qualifications & Scope

• Synthetic Traffic: Traffic flows are produced using Eclipse SUMO microscopic simulations. Vehicle dynamics and lane-changing reflect car-following algorithms, not real-world road behavior.
• Modeled Communication: Packet transfer, radio contact, and queue observations are simulated as an abstract discrete channel with bounded TTL. No Android BLE hardware stack, GATT services, or physical RF fading were executed in this evaluation.
• Assumed Compliance: Driver response to receiving a queue caution is sampled deterministically as a simulation assumption, not observed human behavior.
• No Safety Certification: Simulator collision counters and teleports indicate gridlock resolution triggers and boundary conditions, not safety certifications or crash-reduction claims.
